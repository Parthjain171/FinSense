The Problem

A client asks about their invoice.
An accountant spends an hour responding.

airCFO serves 300+ venture-backed startups. Every invoice question means an accountant manually digging through files, cross-referencing line items, and drafting a reply. FinSense does it in seconds.

Before FinSense

~60 min
  • Open client folder, find the right invoice
  • Read through line items manually
  • Cross-reference with service agreement
  • Check previous months for comparison
  • Calculate any deltas manually
  • Draft a professional reply
  • Review and send

After FinSense

~5 sec
  • Paste the client question
  • Agent retrieves the right invoice via RAG
  • Cross-references automatically
  • Pulls comparison data if needed
  • Calculates metrics with zero LLM cost
  • Drafts a professional reply
  • Human reviews and sends

MCP Tools

6 typed tools. Claude-callable.

Every tool is exposed through the MCP server. External LLM agents can call into FinSense programmatically.

🔍

query_invoices

RAG search over all ingested invoices. Returns the most relevant chunks with relevance scores and source citations.

📈

calculate_metrics

Computes burn rate, runway, gross margin, and month-over-month growth. Pure math, zero LLM cost, zero hallucination.

📋

compare_periods

Compares Q1 vs Q2 or any two time periods. Shows deltas and percentage changes for every expense category.

⚠️

flag_anomalies

Detects unusual month-over-month changes. Catches spikes, drops, and new expenses with severity scoring.

📄

generate_analysis

Produces a CFO-level month-end analysis paragraph from financial data. Revenue, burn, margins, and recommendations.

✉️

draft_reply

Drafts a professional client-facing email reply to an invoice question. A human reviews and sends.
